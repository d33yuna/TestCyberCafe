About 404 Cyber Café

404 Cyber Café is a proposed cyber café located in Melaka, Malaysia. The business is created to serve students, gamers, local residents and anyone who needs a reliable computer and Internet connection.

Our concept focuses on a modern, inclusive and comfortable atmosphere. The space is suitable for gaming, studying, online meetings, assignments, browsing and everyday digital tasks.

Network Design

404 Cyber Café uses a star topology. All wired devices connect to a central 24-port Gigabit switch. The router provides the connection to the Internet, while the wireless access point provides Wi-Fi connectivity and the server provides DNS and HTTP services.
